# Ready-to-send sample emails

Two fully personalized examples of the templates in `email-templates.md`,
one per app, using real companies from the prospect lists. Copy into Gmail,
fill the signature block, add the recipient, send.

## Sample 1 — Gear Wall → KnotsbyAMP (wedding photo & film, Mumbai)

**Subject:** Who has your FX3 right now? — Gear Wall for KnotsbyAMP

Hi team KnotsbyAMP,

I saw you regularly run multiple wedding crews across venues on the same
weekend — which usually means one shared equipment room working overtime
behind the scenes.

When multiple crews share one gear room, the same three things keep happening:
nobody knows who took the camera, two shoots get booked on the same kit, and a
Rs 2,00,000 body goes "missing" until someone checks a cupboard three weeks later.

We built Gear Wall to fix exactly this. It's a simple web app your whole team
uses in the browser:

- The Wall — every camera, lens, light and mic with live status: available,
  in use (by whom, for which shoot, for how long), or damaged.
- Booking calendar with approvals — crews request gear for a date range;
  clashes with other bookings are flagged before they happen; a manager
  approves in one click.
- QR pickup & return — a sticker on each item; scan on the way out, scan on
  the way back. Condition is recorded both ways, overdue returns get flagged
  automatically, and you get a full usage history per item.

It takes about an hour to set up your inventory, works on any phone or laptop,
and your team needs zero training — if they can scan a QR at a restaurant,
they can use this.

a live demo you can click around yourself.

## Sample 2 — Onboarding Academy → Schbang (digital/creative agency, Mumbai)

**Subject:** New joiners productive on day one — Onboarding Academy for Schbang

Hi team Schbang,

With 1,000+ people and constant junior hiring across your campaign teams,
onboarding at your scale is a full-time job that nobody actually has.

Every agency has the same problem: the process lives in a long document nobody
reads, so every new joiner costs a senior person a full day of repeating the
same things — and you still discover the gaps three weeks later, in front of
a client.

We built the Onboarding Academy to fix this. You paste in the processes you've
already written, and it becomes an interactive course:

- Short modules that unlock one by one — each ends with a quick-check
  question, so you know they read it (wrong answers are logged, so you also
  know exactly what they didn't get).
- A final quiz and a real day-one assignment — they do one actual piece of
  work against your checklist and submit it for review.
- Leads review only the gaps — the app shows exactly which checklist items
  failed, you write feedback on those alone, and they resubmit. New joiners
  contribute real work in week one, not week four.

A course takes an afternoon to build in the built-in editor (no IT needed),
and you can run different tracks for designers, writers, and account teams.

try yourself.
