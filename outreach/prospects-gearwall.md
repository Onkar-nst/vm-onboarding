# Gear Wall — Prospect List (India)

Real companies researched from public top-lists, directories and industry articles
(sources at the bottom). Segments: film/TV/OTT production houses, ad-film makers,
corporate video agencies, photography studios, wedding photo & film companies,
event coverage teams, and camera rental houses — every one of them shares expensive
camera gear across multiple crews, which is exactly the problem Gear Wall solves.

**Total: 287 companies — Maharashtra: 107 · Karnataka: 79 · Rest of India: 101**

A note on "top 500": no verified public list of 500 such companies exists per state;
this is every real, relevant company surfaced across ~40 published sources, ranked
lists and directories. The email templates in `email-templates.md` include the
personalization formula to extend this list from the same sources.
