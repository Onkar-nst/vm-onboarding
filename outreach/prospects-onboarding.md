# Onboarding Academy — Prospect List (India)

Real companies researched from public agency rankings, directories and industry
articles (sources at the bottom). Segments: digital marketing, creative/advertising,
social media, performance marketing, branding & design, influencer marketing, PR,
content marketing, video production and creative outsourcing — all hire juniors in
volume and run process-heavy work, which is exactly what the Onboarding Academy fixes.

**Total: 311 companies — Maharashtra: 119 · Karnataka: 73 · Rest of India: 119**

A note on "top 500": no verified public list of 500 such companies exists per state;
this is every real, relevant company surfaced across ~35 published sources and ranked
lists. The templates in `email-templates.md` include the personalization formula to
extend the list from the same sources.
